Hallo!


We are data scientists, and we work for a big online company that sells different products.


Our company wants to offer personalised advertisements to customers based on their characteristics.


They’ve given me a dataset with unlabeled information – specifically, each customer's annual income and a spending score, which the store assigns based on customer behavior and spending habits.


My job is to understand these customers and separate them into different groups based on similar characteristics. Once I’ve identified these groups, I’ll share the results with the marketing team so they can develop targeted advertising strategies for each group.

Considering the objective, what is the most appropriate approach to follow to use this unlabeled data provided by the company?







Because we do not have any labels in the dataset, and since our goal is to cluster customers into different groups based on their similarities, we’ll use an unsupervised learning algorithm to analyze this data.

More specifically, we will use a popular unsupervised learning algorithm called K-means clustering.


So, let's dive into the K-means clustering algorithm and see how it works!
